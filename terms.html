Independent. Thoughtful. Architectural.

Draeven House Lines is a journal for people who believe that the spaces we inhabit shape the lives we lead.

Our story

Draeven House Lines began as a quiet rebellion against the fleeting, image-driven world of design media. We wanted to create a place where architecture and interiors could be discussed with the same rigour and lyricism as literature or art — where a well-proportioned window, a honest material, or a carefully placed lamp could be examined not as trends, but as expressions of how we choose to live.

Founded in 2021 by editor Elena Marchetti, a former interior designer and architecture writer, the publication has grown into an independent journal read by homeowners, architects, designers, and anyone who cares about the built environment. We are entirely reader-supported and free from advertising influence.

“Good design is a quiet act of care — for the people who live in a space and for the world outside it.”

What we believe

Every article we publish is guided by a simple set of principles. We look for work that is honest, enduring, and deeply considered. We value restraint over excess, texture over gloss, and light over noise. And we believe that the best interiors are not those that follow a formula, but those that respond intelligently to the people who use them.

Depth over speed

We publish long-form articles, not listicles. Our writers take the time to understand a subject — whether it’s the chemistry of lime plaster or the psychology of bedroom lighting.

Material honesty

We champion natural, locally sourced materials and craftspeople who understand them. A building should age gracefully, not fight its surroundings.

Human scale

Architecture and interiors are ultimately for people. We focus on proportion, comfort, and the small details that make a space feel like home.

Independent voice

We are not sponsored by brands. Our recommendations come from genuine research and editorial judgement, never from commercial interest.

How we work

We accept pitches from writers and designers who have something original to say. Every article is edited for clarity, accuracy, and style. We fact-check our claims and credit our sources. When we review a product or material, we do so independently — we never accept payment for coverage.

Our readers are our only stakeholders. If you value what we do, the best way to support us is to subscribe to our weekly newsletter and share our articles with others who might enjoy them.
