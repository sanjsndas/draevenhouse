Cookies Policy

We keep things simple. We use only the essential cookies needed to make the site work — nothing more.

Last updated: 15 March 2025 Version 1.8

In short: We use a small number of functional cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to operate. We do not use advertising, tracking, or third‑party analytics cookies. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (they remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (they are deleted when you close your browser).

2. How we use cookies

Draeven House Lines uses only strictly necessary, functional cookies. These are essential for the basic operation of our website, such as:

  • Remembering your cookie consent choice (so we don't ask you again on every page).
  • Maintaining basic security and preventing cross‑site request forgery.
  • Ensuring the website loads correctly and consistently.

We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking, or analytics that identify individual users.

3. Specific cookies we use

The table below lists the cookies that may be set when you visit our site.

Cookie name Purpose Duration Type
cookie_consent Stores your cookie preference so we don't show the banner on every visit. 12 months Functional
session_id Maintains a secure session while you browse the site. Session Strictly necessary
csrf_token Helps protect forms from cross‑site request forgery attacks. Session Strictly necessary

No other cookies are set by us. If you interact with embedded content (e.g., a video), those third‑party services may set their own cookies — please check their policies directly.

4. Your cookie choices

When you first visit our site, you will see a small banner asking you to accept our use of essential cookies. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
  • Block third‑party cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific sites.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note that if you block or delete essential cookies, parts of our website may not function properly.

5. How to control cookies in your browser

You can find instructions for managing cookies in the most common browsers below:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Third‑party services

We do not integrate third‑party analytics, advertising networks, or social media tracking cookies. Our newsletter is delivered via a trusted email service provider, but that only applies once you subscribe and is governed by our Privacy Policy.

If we ever embed content from external platforms (such as a YouTube video), those platforms may set their own cookies. We have no control over these cookies, and we recommend you review the relevant third‑party privacy policies.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to check this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
