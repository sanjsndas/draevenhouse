Frequently asked questions

What makes a well-designed interior? +

Good design balances function, proportion and light. It responds to how people actually live, using materials and layouts that feel intuitive and enduring.

How important is lighting in a room? +

Lighting is everything. It shapes mood, highlights architecture and affects how we feel. A layered approach — ambient, task and accent — is the foundation of a comfortable space.

Can I mix modern and traditional styles? +

Absolutely. The best interiors often blend eras. The key is to find common ground in colour, texture or scale, and to edit carefully so the space feels collected, not chaotic.

Where should I start with a renovation? +

Start with the floor plan and your daily rituals. Understanding how you move through the space will guide every other decision, from materials to furniture placement.
